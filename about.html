About - Anadhya Badri Narayan

A designer with a bias for calm, useful things.

portrait

I'm a graphic designer in Bangalore working across brand identity, packaging and illustration - mostly with small teams making something physical.

My work sits where careful typography meets a light hand with colour. I like a brief with a real object at the end of it: a label, a menu, a printed page. Systems that a two-person team can actually keep using after I've handed them over.

Outside client work I draw - ink and gouache, mostly - and keep a running sketchbook of the city.

“Take one thing away, then look again.”
