About Anadhya Badri Narayan

A designer with a bias for calm, useful things.

I'm a Senior Graphic Designer at Kiana Bespoke Invitations in Bengaluru. Most days I'm working on wedding stationery, event design and the small stories that go with them.

I studied Industrial and Product Design at Raffles Design Institute, so I still think about how something feels in your hands, not just how it looks. Whether it's a wedding suite or a brand, I want the people it's made for to love it and keep it.

Drawing is how I notice things and how I work them out. Almost every project I take on starts in a sketchbook.

“Take one thing away, then look again.”
