How I got to paper

I'm a graphic designer with a background in product design and a love for all things visual.

My work has taken me through branding, illustration, print, stationery and everything in between. I love exploring different styles, experimenting with ideas, and finding thoughtful ways to bring stories to life.

I'm drawn to the little details, meaningful concepts, and the process of turning an idea into something you can hold. Whether it's a brand identity or something intricate and personal, I want the work to feel intentional, timeless and a little unexpected.

Drawing is how I notice things and how I work them out. Almost every project I take on starts in a sketchbook, and my working rule stays the same: take one thing away, then look again.

How a suite gets made

  • Listen first

    A long chat to begin. I look at what already exists and ask what people are actually worried about.

  • Reduce

    Fewer marks, fewer colours and one clear idea. I test it small before anything gets bigger.

  • Make it real

    Print proofs, dielines and files a small team can pick up without me in the room.
