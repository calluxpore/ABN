A designer with a bias for calm, useful things.

I'm a graphic designer with a background in Product Design and a love for all things visual. My creative journey has taken me through branding, illustrations, print, stationery, and everything in between. I love exploring different styles, experimenting with ideas, and finding thoughtful ways to bring stories to life.

I'm drawn to the little details, meaningful concepts, and the process of turning an idea into something tangible. Whether it's building a brand identity or crafting something intricate and personal, I enjoy creating work that feels intentional, timeless, and a little unexpected.

Drawing is how I notice things and how I work them out. Almost every project I take on starts in a sketchbook.

Working rule

“Take one thing away, then look again.”

My working rule.

  • Listen first

    A long chat first. I look at what already exists and ask what people are actually worried about.

  • Reduce

    Fewer marks, fewer colours and one clear idea. I test it small before anything gets bigger.

  • Make it real

    Print proofs, dielines and files a small team can pick up without me in the room.
