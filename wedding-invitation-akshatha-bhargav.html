Akshatha & Bhargav's Wedding Stationery

"Blush florals, gold foil and watercolour." One invitation suite for four events at Le Méridien and Ebisu Palace.

Couple
Akshatha & Bhargav
Venues
Le Méridien & Ebisu Palace
Scope
Cover folio, event cards & motifs
Celebration
Bengaluru, 19 to 22 April 2019

The idea

Soft florals, a bit of gold and a lot of watercolour.

Akshatha Nagendra and Bhargav Sosale had four celebrations in Bengaluru, each with its own mood. I wanted the suite to feel romantic, but still fun enough for the cocktail party and the mehendi.

It opens with a blush folio and a gold foil AB monogram. Inside there's a separate watercolour card for each event at Le Méridien and Ebisu Palace.

  • Peach & blush florals

    Garden roses, eucalyptus and pastel petals around the text.

  • Gold foil monogram

    An AB monogram stamped in gold foil on thick cotton card.

  • Loose watercolour washes

    Turquoise for the cocktail party and sunset orange for the mehendi, painted in gouache.

  • Ebisu Palace & Le Méridien

    A modern hotel and the palace grounds: four days of events that still needed to look like one wedding.

A card for every event

Each event got its own colours and florals, depending on how dressy or relaxed it was.

Foil, flowers and candlelight

All the printed cards together with the rose gold ribbon and a candle, photographed as a keepsake on a wooden table in daylight.
