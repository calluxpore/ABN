# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (and a little vanilla JS), hosted on GitHub Pages at https://calluxpore.github.io/ABN/. No framework or build step. The previous design-tool runtime (`support.js` and its `x-dc` templating) is not to be carried forward.

## Users

Two audiences with equal weight:

- **Employers and design studios** evaluating Anadhya for a role. They want to see range, craft and experience quickly, then check the CV or LinkedIn.
- **Couples and families** who might commission wedding invitations, save the dates or milestone stationery. They want to see finished, printed pieces like the one they need.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Anadhya Badri Narayan, a graphic designer and illustrator in Bengaluru, India. It shows her stationery, illustration and industrial design work. Success is a visitor who leaves convinced of her craft and opens her CV or LinkedIn; a commission enquiry by email is the secondary outcome.

## Positioning

A designer trained in industrial and product design who now draws bespoke wedding and milestone stationery by hand: monograms, motifs and suites built from each family's own traditions (Kerala parasols, Gujarati bandhani, marigold torans, Pichwai cows). Her work is specific to the people it was made for, and some of it is for her own family.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads. Real printed pieces and their stories carry the site; the interface stays out of the way.
2. Specific over generic: name the people, places, motifs and dates each piece was made for.
3. Make the CV and LinkedIn easy to reach from anywhere, with email close behind.
4. Show range: stationery, illustration, characters and industrial design together.
