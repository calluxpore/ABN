Money Heist EN Whitefield Restaurant Branding

“Where every guest becomes a member of the crew”. Character portraits and daily offer graphics for a themed bar and kitchen in Whitefield.

Client Money Heist EN Whitefield
Theme La Casa de Papel
Deliverables Character Portraits & Offer Graphics
Location Whitefield, Bangalore
The Concept

When a bar invites you to join the heist, every detail of the brand has to commit to the plan.

Money Heist EN Whitefield knew exactly what they wanted. The whole place would feel like La Casa de Papel, with the crew as the faces of the brand and little nods to the show everywhere. They needed a full set of character portraits in a flat vector style that would work in print, on Instagram and on the walls.

I started by studying the cast closely. Every portrait has the red jumpsuit, but the small details make each one recognisable, like the Professor's glasses, Tokyo's choker and bob, Nairobi's braids and feather earrings, and the crossed rifles and hard stares of the crew's muscle. Together they became the look of the whole venue.
