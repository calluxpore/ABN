The idea

When a bar invites you to join the heist, every detail of the brand has to commit to the plan.

Money Heist EN Whitefield knew exactly what they wanted. The whole place would feel like La Casa de Papel, with the crew as the faces of the brand and little nods to the show everywhere. They needed a full set of character portraits in a flat vector style that would work in print, on Instagram and on the walls.

I started by studying the cast closely. Every portrait has the red jumpsuit, but the small details make each one recognisable, like the Professor's glasses, Tokyo's choker and bob, Nairobi's braids and feather earrings, and the crossed rifles and hard stares of the crew's muscle.

What's drawn in

  • Flat vector portraits

    Every character drawn from scratch in clean flat vector, costume, attitude and all.

  • The red jumpsuit

    The red jumpsuit and Dali mask tie the whole crew together and set the colours for the brand.

  • Offer graphics

    Offers named after the crew, like Moscow Mondays, Oslo Wednesdays and Tokio Thursdays.

  • Money everywhere

    Flying banknotes and the Dali mask show up on everything, printed or online.

Printed, with a gold charm

The Professor cards were printed as a set, each with his portrait on the front, a gold chain charm and a small name tag. The gold butterfly sticker makes them feel like something you'd want to keep.

I shot them on grass so the white card really stands out. It makes them look like a little gift, which suits the place.
