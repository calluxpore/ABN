Restaurant Branding : Money Heist EN Whitefield

“Where every guest becomes a member of the crew” : A full character illustration suite and branded day offer graphics for a boldly themed bar and kitchen in Whitefield.

Client Money Heist EN Whitefield
Theme La Casa de Papel Universe
Deliverables Character Portraits & Offer Graphics
Location Whitefield, Bangalore
The Concept

When a bar invites you to join the heist, every detail of the brand has to commit to the plan.

Money Heist EN Whitefield approached the brief with a clear vision: the restaurant would embody the world of La Casa de Papel, with the crew as brand ambassadors and every touch point a nod to the show. The brief called for a full set of character portraits that felt authentically rooted in the flat vector aesthetic of the series, translated into a cohesive illustration language suited for print, digital, and in venue display.

Starting from detailed character study of the original cast, each portrait was built in a bold, graphic flat vector style capturing the recognisable red jumpsuit, the individual personality cues, and the attitude. The Professor received his signature chain and character card finish, Tokyo her choker and bob cut, Nairobi her braided pigtails and feather earrings, and the muscle of the crew their crossed rifles and intense stares. The result is a crew portrait gallery that doubles as a visual identity for the venue.

Fanned character cards with gold charm and label finish

The Professor character cards were printed as a fanned set, each bearing the character illustration on the front with a gold chain charm and a small character label card. The butterfly gold sticker adds a collectible quality to the printed piece.

Shot against natural green grass to contrast the clean white card stock, the flatlay communicates a premium, giftable feel appropriate for the venue's immersive theme positioning.
