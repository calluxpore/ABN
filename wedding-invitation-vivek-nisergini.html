Vivek & Nisergini's Wedding Invitation

"Two traditions, one invite." A suite for a Kerala and Maharashtrian wedding, with banana leaves, Muthukkuda umbrellas and a monogram of their initials.

Couple
Vivek & Nisergini
Traditions
Kerala & Maharashtrian
Scope
Monogram, print & motif suite
Destinations
Mumbai & Pookkottukavu, Kerala

The idea

Two very different wedding traditions, and one invitation that had to feel right to both families.

Vivek and Nisergini had two ceremonies back to back in Mumbai, a Kerala Muhurtham in the morning and then the Maharashtrian wedding. A week later there was a reception back home in Pookkottukavu, Kerala.

The design leans on symbols both families would recognise, like the golden Muthukkuda (the parasol carried in temple processions) with its beaded tassels, banana leaves for a good start, and a cursive monogram joining their initials.

  • The Muthukkuda parasol

    The gold silk umbrellas with beaded tassels you see in Kerala temple processions.

  • Plantain foliage (vazhayila)

    Banana leaves drawn by hand around the edges of the cards, a sign of plenty and of the wedding feast.

  • Intertwined NV monogram

    A cursive N and V joined together in deep green, sitting on top of the parasol.

  • Warm ivory paper & saffron

    Warm card stock with saffron headings, classic serif type and Sanskrit blessings.

Cover and inserts

Three cards: the cover, the Mumbai wedding and the Kerala reception.
