Dhriti & Prashant's Save the Date

“Teasing the main event”. A save the date for a wedding that brings Gujarati and Telugu families together.

Couple Dhriti & Prashant
Theme Gujarati and Telugu
Format Animated Teaser & Artwork
Destination Hyderabad, Dec 17 to 18, 2026
The Concept

A Save the Date doesn't need to reveal the whole story, just enough to make you curious about what's coming.

Starting with the moodboard, I wanted to move away from the usual Save the Date format and create something that felt more like a visual teaser for the wedding itself.

Since the celebration brings together North and South Indian traditions, I picked out recognisable details from both cultures, including banana leaves, brass lamps, florals, marigolds, draped textiles, architecture and festive décor, and brought them together as individual fragments rather than one complete scene.
