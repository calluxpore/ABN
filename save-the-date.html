Dhriti & Prashant's Save the Date

"Teasing the main event." A save the date for a wedding that brings Gujarati and Telugu families together.

Couple
Dhriti & Prashant
Traditions
Gujarati and Telugu
Format
Animated teaser & artwork
Destination
Hyderabad, 17 & 18 Dec 2026

The idea

A save the date doesn't need to reveal the whole story, just enough to make you curious about what's coming.

Starting with the moodboard, I wanted to move away from the usual save the date format and make something that felt more like a visual teaser for the wedding itself.

Since the celebration brings together North and South Indian traditions, I picked out recognisable details from both cultures, including banana leaves, brass lamps, florals, marigolds, draped textiles, architecture and festive décor, and brought them together as individual fragments rather than one complete scene.

  • Banana leaves & brass lamps

    Two things you see at every Telugu wedding, so they had to be in there.

  • Marigolds & draped textiles

    Bandhani prints, mogra garlands and plenty of gold from the Gujarati side.

  • Arches & the mandap

    Arches borrowed from Hyderabad's old buildings, framing the moment the date appears.

  • Doves & temple bells

    White doves and swinging bells to keep the whole thing light and happy.

The animated teaser

In the animation each piece drops into the frame one at a time. The bells swing, the fabric settles and the DP monogram comes together before the city is revealed.

It's made in a 9:16 vertical format so both families could share it easily on WhatsApp and Instagram stories.
