The Concept

A Save the Date doesn't need to reveal the whole story - just enough to make you curious about what's coming.

Starting with the moodboard, I wanted to move away from the usual Save the Date format and create something that felt more like a visual teaser for the wedding itself.

Since the celebration brings together North and South Indian traditions, I picked out recognisable details from both cultures - banana leaves, brass lamps, florals, marigolds, draped textiles, architecture and festive décor - and brought them together as individual fragments rather than one complete scene.
