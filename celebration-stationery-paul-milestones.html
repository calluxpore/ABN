Celebration Stationery : The Paul & Milestone Suites

“Cherishing life milestones through thoughtful design” : A curated collection of bespoke invitation suites celebrating betrothals, new homes, and new beginnings.

Occasions Betrothal, Homam & Baby Shower
Celebrants Cicilla & Wilbur, Tara, Ananya
Design Scope Invitations, Seals & Illustration
Locations Bengaluru & Ardmore Papakura
The Concept

Honouring life's most intimate milestones through personalized typography, traditional motifs, and playful illustrations.

Across life's most meaningful milestones, stationery acts as the tactile first whisper of a celebration. This curated collection brings together bespoke invitations created for intimate family gatherings, from a formal betrothal dinner at The Paul hotel in Bengaluru to sacred housewarming rituals and joyous baby shower announcements.

Each design is approached as a reflection of the occasion's unique spirit, pairing refined serif typography and gold seal monograms for formal banquets with sacred Pichwai cows and vibrant balloon arches for welcoming home rituals and new arrivals.
