The Paul & Milestone Invitations

"For the big days in between." Invitations for an engagement, a new home and a few new babies.

Occasions
Betrothal, homam & baby showers
Celebrants
Cicilla & Wilbur, Tara, Ananya
Scope
Invitations, seals & illustration
Locations
Bengaluru & Ardmore, Papakura

The idea

Smaller family occasions, each with its own personality.

An invitation is usually the first thing guests hold before a celebration. These are a few I made for family occasions, from a formal betrothal dinner at The Paul in Bengaluru to a housewarming pooja and some very happy baby showers.

I tried to match each one to the mood of the day. The dinner got serif type and gold seals, the housewarming got Pichwai cows, and the babies got balloons.

  • Betrothal at The Paul

    Cursive lettering and gold seals for Cicilla and Wilbur's betrothal dinner at The Paul, Bengaluru.

  • Pichwai cows & homam

    Temple arches, Kamadhenu cows and pink lotus vines for a new home blessing.

  • Watercolour roses

    Rose garlands and soft blue washes for a baby shower.

  • Playful nursery art

    Pastel balloons, a little crib and soft toys, all drawn by hand.

Seven occasions

From a dinner in the city to a homam at home, every card is a little different.
