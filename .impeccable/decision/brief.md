# Portfolio site (all pages)

Scope: home, about and the six case studies. Mode: Experience. Audience: employers and studios, and couples and families, with equal weight. Main action: open the CV or LinkedIn; email second. Visitor asked for: name and role first, an index of everything, nothing template-like, dark, over-decorated, or hiding the CV.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is the thick sample album a Bengaluru card shop hands you across the counter: a linen cover, a fore-edge of tabs, one design per page. It refuses the white thumbnail grid. The user pinned a neutral palette (beige, whites and greys only), so the album is bound in beige linen and the colour comes only from her work.

OWN-WORLD: Beige linen (#DDD3C4) for the cover and tab rail ground, white album pages, warm charcoal (#2B2926) ink and buttons, tabs in white, pale beige and warm grey by discipline, every picture, page, tab and button with rounded corners (user pinned). Rozha One for stamped display, Hind for everything read. No gradients, and no shadows at rest; a picture tilting under the pointer casts a theme-aware shadow (user pinned). No hue outside the photographs.

STORY: In one screen the visitor reads who she is and what she does, sees every project as a tab, and finds the CV. Pages below prove the craft with real printed pieces; case studies open each design's page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed beige linen cover. Top bar: ABN mark left; Work, About, LinkedIn, and a charcoal "CV (PDF)" button right. Name stamped in charcoal at wall size, role beneath. Centre: the newest suite, a rounded picture, as the cover's window. Right edge: twelve stacked tabs, the index; hovering a tab pulls it out and swaps its picture into the window.

FORM: The Card Shop Sample Album, position 1 on my ordered list (chosen as Impeccable's pick). seed:07ea191ded00a707

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction and motion

The tab rail is the navigation on every page: on the cover a hovered or focused tab slides out from the fore-edge and shows that project's cover; on case pages the current project's tab stays pulled out. Pages turn in: each album page enters with a short lift as it scrolls in; reduced motion shows everything at rest.
