Shashtiabdapoorthi : Sri. Sampath Badri Narayan

“Honouring sixty years of grace” : An heirloom milestone stationery suite designed by Anadhya for her beloved father’s 60th milestone birthday and parental vow renewal at Bangalore Palace.

Celebrants Sri. Sampath & Smt. Jayashree
Occasion 60th Birthday & Mangalyadharanam
Design Scope Cover Folio, Inserts & Motifs
Venue Tennis Pavilion, Bangalore Palace
The Concept

A personal tribute uniting Vedic rituals, festive warmth, and royal heritage.

In South Indian Vedic traditions, Shashtiabdapoorthi marks the milestone completion of sixty years : celebrating longevity, spiritual wisdom, and the resolemnization of marital bonds through Mangalyadharanam.

Designed by Anadhya alongside her family for her father Sri. Sampath Badri Narayan, this bespoke invitation suite pairs handcrafted traditional motifs : vibrant marigold torans, bronze temple bells, and emerald Indian ringneck parrots : with elegant classical serif typography on warm, tactile textured stock for their celebration at Bangalore Palace.

🌼

Marigold Toran (Chendumalli)

Strings of golden-yellow and saffron marigolds cascading across the cards, symbolizing auspicious divine light and renewal.

🔔

Sacred Temple Bells (Mani)

Suspended bronze ritual bells with foaming sacred milk offerings, evoking the uplifting reverberations of Vedic chants.

🦜

Indian Ringneck Parrots (Kili)

Graceful emerald parrots perched amongst the floral garlands, an auspicious symbol of devotion, family joy, and eloquence.

🏛️

Bangalore Palace Setting

Set against the historic Tennis Pavilion at Bangalore Palace, pairing classical heritage with intimate celebratory grandeur.
