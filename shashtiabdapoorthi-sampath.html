Shashtiabdapoorthi for Sri. Sampath Badri Narayan

"Honouring sixty years." The invitations I designed for my father's sixtieth birthday and my parents' vow renewal at Bangalore Palace.

Celebrants
Sri. Sampath & Smt. Jayashree
Occasion
60th birthday & Mangalyadharanam
Scope
Cover folio, inserts & motifs
Venue
Tennis Pavilion, Bangalore Palace

The idea

A very personal project, for a very big day in our family.

In South Indian Vedic traditions, Shashtiabdapoorthi marks the completion of sixty years. It celebrates a long life, and the couple renew their marriage vows through Mangalyadharanam.

I designed this with my family for my father, Sri. Sampath Badri Narayan. It pairs traditional motifs we grew up with, like marigold torans, bronze temple bells and green ringneck parrots, with classic serif type on warm textured paper.

  • Marigold toran (chendumalli)

    Strings of yellow and saffron marigolds running across the cards, for light and new beginnings.

  • Temple bells (mani)

    Bronze bells and milk offerings, the sounds and sights of the pooja.

  • Ringneck parrots (kili)

    Green parrots sitting in the garlands, a lucky sign for love and a happy family.

  • Bangalore Palace

    The celebration was at the Tennis Pavilion in Bangalore Palace, so the cards needed to feel a little grand.

Folio and inserts

The cover, the invitation and the programme for both days.
